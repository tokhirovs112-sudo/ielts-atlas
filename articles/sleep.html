Why Do We Need to Sleep?

Published October 23, 2026 · 3-minute read

Sleep is very important for our health. Every night, we go to bed and close our eyes. But why do we need to sleep?

First, sleep gives our bodies energy. After a long day at school or work, we feel tired. When we sleep, our bodies rest. In the morning, we feel ready for a new day.

Second, sleep helps our brains. When we sleep, our brains work with the things we learn during the day. This helps us remember new words, facts, and ideas. Good sleep also helps us think and learn better.

Sleep is vital for our bodies, brains, and emotions

Sleep is also important for our feelings. When we do not sleep enough, we may feel angry, sad, or tired. It can be hard to listen to other people or do our homework.

How many hours should we sleep? Most teenagers need about 8–10 hours of sleep every night. Adults usually need about 7–9 hours.

There are some easy ways to sleep better. Go to bed at the same time every night. Put your phone away before sleeping. Make your bedroom quiet and comfortable.

Remember, sleep is not a waste of time. It helps our bodies rest, our brains learn, and our minds feel better. So, sleep well and be ready for a great day!
