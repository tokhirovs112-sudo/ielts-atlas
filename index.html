ORIGINAL PRACTICE

Reading practice · The urban garden

Read the passage, then check your answers.

In several cities, unused rooftops are being transformed into community gardens. Residents grow vegetables, share gardening skills, and create green spaces in crowded neighbourhoods. These gardens can help people access fresh produce, although their success depends on reliable water supplies and volunteers who can look after the plants. Some projects also offer workshops for children, teaching them where food comes from and why soil health matters. Garden organisers say that starting small and choosing crops suited to the local climate often makes a project easier to maintain.

Questions 1–5 · Choose one answer
1. What are some unused rooftops being turned into?
2. What can gardens help residents access?
3. Which two things help a garden succeed?
4. Who may attend the educational workshops?
5. What do organisers recommend when starting?

Choose an answer for each question.

Answer key: 1. Community gardens · 2. Fresh produce · 3. Reliable water and volunteers · 4. Children · 5. Start small and choose crops suited to the local climate.

This passage and these questions were written originally for IELTS Atlas; they are not an official IELTS test.

Listening practice · Booking a workshop

Woman: Hello, Greenfield Community Centre. How can I help? Man: Hi, I'd like to book a place on the Saturday photography workshop. Woman: Certainly. It starts at ten thirty and finishes at twelve thirty. Man: Great. How much does it cost? Woman: It's fifteen pounds, and that includes the printed guide. Please bring your own camera. Man: I have a camera. Do I need to register online? Woman: You can register on our website, or come to reception before Friday afternoon. Man: I'll do it online tonight. Thank you. Woman: You're welcome. We'll see you on Saturday.

Questions 1–5

Choose the correct answer based on what you hear.

1. Which workshop does the man want to attend?
2. On which day is the workshop?
3. What time does it start?
4. How much does it cost?
5. What should the man bring?

Listen carefully and choose your answers.

Answer key: 1. Photography · 2. Saturday · 3. 10:30 · 4. £15 · 5. His own camera.
