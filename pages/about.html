ABOUT ENGLISITY

Free IELTS practice with authentic, recent questions

Englisity was started in March 2026 by Tokhirov Saidalokhon to help students prepare better for IELTS with more authentic questions, and to keep all of it free.

Why Englisity exists

Many practice materials are old or far from what candidates really see on test day. Englisity collects questions that test-takers have recently reported and turns them into practice you can do straight away, with timers, answer checking and vocabulary help.

Where the questions come from

Speaking and Writing questions are reported by people who have just taken the test. The “Last reported” date shows when a question was last seen.

Englisity is an independent, unofficial practice site. It is not connected to or endorsed by IELTS, the British Council, IDP or Cambridge.

Help other students

Took the test recently? Share the questions you got, so the next students can practise them. Found a wrong answer, a bad definition or something that does not work? Tell us and it will be fixed.
