Speaking room

Practise answering clearly, supporting your ideas and speaking naturally.

Speaking sections

1

Part 1 · Interview

Answer familiar questions about study, hobbies and everyday life.

Try: What do you enjoy doing in your free time?
2

Part 2 · Cue card

Prepare a short talk using a simple plan: who, what, when, why.

Try: Describe a skill you would like to learn.
3

Part 3 · Discussion

Explain opinions, compare ideas and give reasons or examples.

Try: How has technology changed education?

Simple speaking plan

Answer the question directly. Give one reason. Add a specific example. Finish with a short concluding thought.
