Academic Writing Task 1

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words. Your response should be long enough to cover the main features clearly.
Word count: 0 150-word minimum

Academic Writing Task 2

Write an essay in response to the question. Support your ideas with explanations and relevant examples.

Write at least 250 words. Your response should develop a clear position and support it with relevant explanations and examples.

Word count: 0 250-word minimum
